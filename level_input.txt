TITLE: Cave of Dread
DIFFICULTY: Medium Demon
LENGTH: Long
SONG_ID: 763248

DESCRIPTION:
This level is set in a dark, crumbling cave filled with glowing crystals, ancient ruins, and danger at every turn. The player must traverse through all gameplay modes — cube, ship, ball, UFO, wave, robot, and spider — with lots of spammy, fast-paced sections. A large cave monster follows the player from the background, gradually getting closer, while other smaller monsters shoot fireballs toward the player using move + rotate triggers.

STRUCTURE:
- 0s–10s: Cube mode — spikes and fake blocks, subtle monster shadow appears behind
- 10s–20s: Ship — narrow cave tunnels with saws swinging
- 20s–30s: Ball — anti-gravity crystal field, tight spam clicks
- 30s–40s: Wave — jagged cave walls and sharp crystal spikes
- 40s–50s: UFO — floating platforms, monster arms reach across screen
- 50s–60s: Robot — precision jumps on cracked stones
- 60s–70s: Spider — teleport portals, rapid-fire clicks
- 70s–80s: Dual — wave + cube, cave collapse animations
- 80s–End: Final cube escape — monster charges, fireballs fly, cave collapses

VISUALS:
- Background: Dark grey/blue cave texture
- Ground: Cracked stone with red crystal highlights
- Colors: Red, black, violet, and electric blue
- Monster: Black silhouette with red glowing eyes, appears from right and moves toward player using move triggers
- Fireballs: Red-orange glowing orbs with rotate/move loop, triggered by monsters off-screen

OBJECTS:
- Move Trigger: Monster movement and fireball launching
- Pulse Trigger: Every fireball impact causes screen shake
- Animate Trigger: Monster growl and blinking eyes
- Spawn Trigger: Used for sequenced monster attacks

NOTES:
- Add custom object groups for "Monster", "Fireball", and "Cave Debris"
- Make heavy use of invisible blocks to control fireball paths
- Use camera shake for fireball impact moments
